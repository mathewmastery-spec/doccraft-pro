## Information processed

DocCraft Pro may process:
- Email address and optional name/country information supplied to identify a credit wallet.
- Text and structured information entered into a document-generation request, and the generated document content returned by the service.
- Credit-wallet balances, credit ledger entries, package purchases, payment references, transaction amounts/currencies, and payment status.
- Technical request identifiers and operational logs needed to protect, troubleshoot, and operate the service.

Do not submit information you do not have permission to process. Avoid entering highly sensitive information unless the service explicitly supports it and you understand the associated risks.

## How information is used

Information is used to generate requested documents, maintain a credit wallet, initialize and verify purchases, prevent duplicate credit grants, troubleshoot failures, and maintain service security.

Document-generation requests are sent to the configured AI service provider to generate the requested output. Payment details needed for checkout and verification are sent to Paystack or another payment provider explicitly configured by the operator. DocCraft Pro should not collect or store payment-card PINs or mobile-money account passwords.

## Storage, processors, and sharing

Account, generation, wallet, and transaction records are stored in the application's configured database. The hosting provider processes application traffic and infrastructure data. The AI provider processes generation inputs and outputs; the payment provider processes payment transactions. Each provider's own privacy terms also apply.

**Before publication, the operator must identify the actual hosting, database, AI, and payment providers in use, check their current data-processing terms, and accurately describe any other integrations.**
